How to Become a BoloUp Go Host

1

Step 1 – Download and Create Your Account

Download BoloUp and register using one of the available sign-up methods.

2

Step 2 – Open Become Host

Go to your profile, find Become Host, and tap Join to start the host registration process.

3

Step 3 – Enter Your Agency ID

Open the Join Agency form and enter the Agency ID (2525968) provided by the agency you want to join. Check the agency details before submitting.

4

Step 4 – Complete Face Verification

Go to the Authentication Center and follow the on-screen instructions to complete your face verification.

5

Step 5 – Complete Your Host Application

After your registration and verification have been reviewed and approved, your account can begin operating as a BoloUp host.

How to Withdraw Your BoloUp Go Host Earnings

1

Step 1 – Open Your Profile

Log in to BoloUp Go and head to your profile page.

2

Step 2 – Tap Diamonds

Open Diamonds to see your eligible balance and payout settings.

3

Step 3 – Choose Withdraw Method

Select Withdraw Method and review the payout options currently available in your region.

4

Step 4 – Connect Your Payout Account

Open Withdrawal Info and enter the required bank, e-wallet, Payoneer, USDT, or other supported payout details shown in your account.

5

Step 5 – Enter Your Withdrawal Amount

Choose how much you want to withdraw. Your balance needs to meet the current minimum withdrawal requirement.

6

Step 6 – Review and Withdraw

Double-check your payout information and amount, then submit your withdrawal request.
